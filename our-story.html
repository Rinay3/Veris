Our Foundation

Born in the
City of Diamonds

Surat has been the world's diamond cutting capital for centuries. Over 90% of the world's rough diamonds are cut and polished here by artisans whose skills pass from generation to generation.

Véris was founded to honor this heritage while building on it — pairing ancient Surat craftsmanship with 21st-century CVD laboratory science to create diamonds that are physically and chemically identical to mined stones, but free of human cost.

Science of Brilliance

The CVD Process

Six steps that turn carbon gas into the world's rarest mineral — without a single mine.

01
Seed Crystal Preparation

A microscopically thin diamond seed crystal — often a small slice of an existing diamond — is precisely cut, polished, and placed inside a vacuum plasma reactor chamber. This seed will become the foundation of the new diamond.

02
Plasma Ignition

A mixture of hydrogen and methane gas is introduced into the chamber. Microwave energy creates a high-energy plasma ball reaching temperatures exceeding 3,000°C — replicating the intense heat of Earth's mantle in a controlled laboratory environment.

03
Carbon Deposition

Carbon atoms from the methane plasma rain down layer by layer onto the diamond seed at an atomic level, bonding in an exact crystalline lattice identical to a natural diamond. Growth progresses at approximately 0.006mm per hour — an act of extraordinary patience.

04
Rough Diamond Harvest

After 2–4 weeks of growth, the rough CVD diamond crystal is carefully removed from the chamber. At this stage, it resembles a rough natural crystal — unpolished, complex, and carbon-pure. Our gemologists assess its yield and quality grade.

05
Master Cutting in Surat

The rough is transported to our Surat atelier where fourth-generation diamond cutters plan and execute each cut using 3D optical mapping software. A single Round Brilliant requires 58 precisely angled facets, optimized for maximum light return.

06
IGI Certification

Every finished stone is sent to the International Gemological Institute for independent grading. Each report documents Cut, Color, Clarity, and Carat with a unique laser-inscribed serial number invisible to the naked eye. Your certificate of ethical origin.

Scientific Verification

IGI Certification
Explained

diamond
Cut

Evaluates the proportions and symmetry of each facet. Our stones are graded Excellent or Ideal — the top 3% of all diamonds cut worldwide.

palette
Color

Measured on D–Z scale. D, E, F are colorless — the rarest. Véris's CVD process produces predominantly D–F range stones due to the controlled purity of the growth environment.

center_focus_strong
Clarity

Assesses internal (inclusions) and external (blemishes) characteristics. Our standard is VS1–VVS2: inclusions visible only under 10x magnification, invisible to the naked eye.

scale
Carat

The metric weight of the diamond. One carat equals 0.2 grams. Because CVD diamonds grow in controlled conditions, weight is precisely calibrated — no yield loss from mining imperfections.
